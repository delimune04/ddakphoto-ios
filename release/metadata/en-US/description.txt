Need a smaller photo for an upload?
DdakPhoto converts your photos to JPG within your chosen file size and image size limits.

Select photos, set your limits, convert, and save or share.
Process up to 20 photos in one batch.

Features
• Set a maximum file size in KB
• Optionally limit the longest edge of each photo
• Convert supported photos, including HEIC, to JPG
• Compare original and converted file sizes and result dimensions
• Save or share individual photos or an entire batch

Photos are converted on your device. They are not sent to the app developer's server.
Results are new files; original photos in your library are never edited.
Original EXIF information and capture location are not copied to the output files.

One purchase unlocks the app's features.
No ads, subscriptions, additional in-app purchases, or account registration.

Please note
• Compression may reduce image quality or dimensions.
• JPG does not support transparent backgrounds.
• Animated photos are converted to still images.
• In this app, 1 KB equals 1,000 bytes.
• Upload requirements vary. Check your results against the destination's requirements before submitting.
• Selecting an original stored only in iCloud may require Apple Photos to download it.

An iPhone app requiring iOS 17 or later. The app interface is currently available in Korean.
